Roswell First

All of Roswell, Georgia

Roswell is home base. The service area covers every part of the city across ZIP codes 30075 and 30076. That runs from the historic district around Canton Street, Mimosa Boulevard and Bulloch Hall to 1978 to 1995 subdivisions such as Horseshoe Bend, Martin's Landing, Brookfield Country Club and Edenwilde, plus the townhome communities along Holcomb Bridge Road. The small city of Mountain Park, which is served by the Roswell Water Utility along with parts of Roswell, is covered too.

Inside Roswell, the first practical question is which water provider serves the address. The Roswell Water Utility covers roughly 18,500 residents in and around downtown, and Fulton County supplies the rest. Fulton County runs the sewer everywhere in the city. Your bill says which one you have, and the leak detection and water line pages explain what that means when something leaks near the meter.

Neighboring Towns

What Is Different About Each Town

Milton

Estate and equestrian lots, many on septic. Slow drains can mean a tank problem, not a clog.

Sandy Springs

Water from Atlanta, sewer from Fulton, and older homes with aging supply lines.

Johns Creek

Late-1980s and 1990s planned communities, many now on their third water heater.

Dunwoody and East Cobb

Dunwoody runs on DeKalb water with an aging grid. East Cobb's 1970s laterals sit under mature trees.

Peachtree Corners

Gwinnett water and sewer, a 1967 planned community around Technology Park, reached straight down Holcomb Bridge Road.

Each town page covers local housing, utility contacts, ZIP codes and the problems most worth watching for there.

ZIP Codes We Cover

The core service area covers Roswell's 30075 and 30076, Milton's 30004 and 30009, Johns Creek's 30097, 30022 and 30005, Sandy Springs' 30328, 30350, 30342 and 30327, Dunwoody's 30338, 30346 and 30360, East Cobb's 30062, 30066, 30067 and 30068, and Peachtree Corners' 30092, 30071 and 30096.

Each town page also lists the additional ZIPs within about 10 miles of that town, which reach into parts of Alpharetta, Duluth, Norcross, Chamblee, Brookhaven, Marietta, Smyrna and Woodstock. Smaller communities such as Crabapple, which sits mostly inside Milton, and Mountain Park are covered without separate pages. If your ZIP is not listed anywhere, call (770) 214-4545 and ask. Emergency burst pipe repair and drain cleaning are available around the clock across the whole area.

Plumbing work in every one of these towns falls under Georgia's state minimum plumbing code, adapted from the IPC, with permits issued by each city or county. This is Roswell, Georgia, in Fulton County. We do not serve Roswell, New Mexico.
