Willow Springs

Around the Country Club of Roswell

Willow Springs surrounds the Country Club of Roswell in East Roswell, between Old Alabama Road and Haynes Bridge Road, less than 2 miles from GA-400. Neighborhood guides count about 855 homes, mostly built in the late 1970s and 1980s, with ranch and traditional houses on large, tree-lined lots.

Smaller-lot sections such as Camden Glen and Kinross sit within the neighborhood. A small gated townhome community, built in 2005, lines Old Alabama Road. Johns Creek's Newtown Park is less than 2 miles east.

The mailing addresses can be confusing. Some Willow Springs listings use ZIP 30022 with an Alpharetta mailing address. According to the Census Bureau's geocoder, addresses on Club Springs Drive, Kinross Road and a Haynes Bridge Road address with an Alpharetta 30022 mailing label all fall inside the City of Roswell.

Why the Mailing Address Matters for Plumbing

A mailing ZIP is a postal label, not a city boundary. For permits, the city that matters is the one your home actually sits in, and for these streets that is Roswell. Ask your plumber to confirm the jurisdiction before a permit is pulled for a water heater, a sewer repair or a new gas line.

The water provider works the same way. Fulton County is the more likely provider for these East Roswell streets, and the bill shows which one serves you.
