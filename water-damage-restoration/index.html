Water Damage Restoration

After the Pipe Is Fixed, the Water Is Still in the House

Water damage restoration is the work of returning a house to its pre-loss condition after water has soaked into it. That means extracting standing water, finding where moisture has traveled, drying the structure with commercial equipment, removing materials that cannot be saved, and rebuilding. It is separate from plumbing repair. The plumber stops the leak. Restoration deals with everything the leak touched.

In Roswell, the causes range from ordinary to historic. A burst supply line in an upstairs bath can soak two floors in an hour. During the December 2022 holiday freeze, Roswell fire crews logged more than 100 burst-pipe calls in a single day, according to Atlanta News First. In September 2009, the USGS measured a 100-year flood on the Chattahoochee at Roswell, and homes along Riverside Road took on water.

Water Damage Emergency: What to Do in the First Hour

These steps limit the damage and protect your insurance claim.

  • 1
    Stop the water. Close the main shutoff. If the source is a plumbing failure, the burst pipe repair page covers the repair itself.
  • 2
    Cut power to wet areas. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out of the area and call your electric utility.
  • 3
    Photograph everything. Take wide shots and close-ups of the water, the source and damaged belongings before moving anything.
  • 4
    Move what you can. Lift furniture off wet carpet and move electronics, documents and rugs to a dry area.
  • 5
    Call your insurer. Report the loss early and ask about their process and any documentation they need.

Do not use a household vacuum on standing water, and stay out of any water from a sewer backup.

Water Categories

Clean, Gray or Contaminated: Why the Source Matters

The industry classifies water by contamination level, which decides what can be dried and what has to go.

CategoryTypical Roswell sourceWhat it means for cleanup
Category 1: cleanBurst supply line, water heater leak, overflowing sinkMost materials can be dried in place if work starts quickly
Category 2: grayWashing machine or dishwasher overflow, toilet overflow without fecesCarpet pad and some porous materials are often removed
Category 3: contaminatedSewer backup, creek or river flooding, long-standing waterPorous materials removed, stricter cleaning procedures

Clean water does not stay clean. Water that sits for a day or two in a warm house can degrade toward a higher category, which is one more reason to start drying fast.

The Process

How Restoration Moves From Wet to Rebuilt

  1. 1

    Inspect and map

    Moisture meters and thermal cameras show where water traveled.

  2. 2

    Extract

    Remove standing water from floors, carpet and pad.

  3. 3

    Dry and monitor

    Air movers and dehumidifiers, with daily moisture readings.

  4. 4

    Remove and rebuild

    Replace materials that could not be saved.

The industry reference for this work is the IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration. It covers how to assess water categories, set up drying equipment and verify that materials are actually dry. Ask any restoration provider how their process lines up with it and how they document moisture readings.
