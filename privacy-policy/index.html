Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information 247plumberroswell.com collects and how it is used. The site is operated under the name Roswell 24/7 Plumbing, 950 3rd St, Suite 21, Roswell, GA. Questions go to contact@247plumberroswell.com.

Information We Collect

This site has no forms, accounts or checkout. We do not ask you to type in personal information. When you call the phone number shown on the site, the call is handled by a call-tracking and routing service. That service records basic call data such as your phone number, the time and length of the call, and the page that showed the number. We may record calls so we can review how they were handled.

Like most websites, our hosting provider logs standard technical data when you visit, such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed. We use this only to keep the site running and secure and to understand which pages are useful.

Third-Party Services

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts and embed maps from Google Maps. Those services may set cookies or collect technical data under Google's own privacy policy. We do not control that collection.

How Information Is Used and Shared

Call information is used to connect you with an independent local plumbing provider and to measure which pages produce calls. It is shared with that provider so they can return your call and perform the service you request. Your personal information is never sold.

Your Choices

You can browse this site without calling. You can block cookies in your browser settings, although embedded maps may not display. To ask about information tied to a call you made, email contact@247plumberroswell.com.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging home or business services. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Policy written and reviewed by the Roswell 24/7 Plumbing team. Questions about data from a call go to contact@247plumberroswell.com.
