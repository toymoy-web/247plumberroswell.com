What to Have Ready When You Call

  • Your address and nearest cross street in Roswell or the nearby town.
  • What you see: where the water is, which fixtures are affected, any smell or noise.
  • What you have already shut off, if anything.
  • Whether your water comes from the Roswell Water Utility or Fulton County, if you know.
  • The approximate age of the house, which helps us guess the pipe material before we arrive.
