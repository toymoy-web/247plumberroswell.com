Sewer Line Repair

The Pipe Fulton County Leaves to You

Sewer line repair means fixing the private sewer lateral that carries wastewater from your house to the public main. In Roswell, every one of those mains is run by Fulton County, and every lateral connected to them belongs to the property owner. The County's service lateral flyer spells it out: if the lateral clogs, leaks or breaks anywhere between the house and the main, the owner is responsible, even where the pipe runs under the street or through an easement.

Most people learn this during their first backup, standing in a wet basement. The better news is that the repair is often smaller than it first looks. A single cracked joint or a short root-filled stretch can be fixed without replacing the whole line, as long as someone finds it precisely.

Sewer Emergency: Sewage Backing Up Into the House

A sewer backup is a health emergency as well as a plumbing one. Raw sewage carries bacteria, and it soaks into anything porous. Do these things right away, then call the emergency line.

  1. Stop all water use. No flushing, laundry, dishwasher or showers. Every gallon has to go somewhere.
  2. Keep people and pets away. Close the door to the affected room and keep children out.
  3. Find the cleanout. If you know where the outdoor cleanout cap is, tell the dispatcher. Do not open it yourself.
  4. Check with neighbors. If the house next door is backing up too, the problem may be the County main.

If the County main is the cause, Fulton County's sewer line handles it. If it is your lateral, that is the emergency call we take. Call (770) 214-4545.

Warning Signs

Signs the Problem Is the Pipe, Not a Clog

A clog is something inside the pipe. A sewer line problem is something wrong with the pipe. From indoors they look alike, but a few signs lean clearly toward damage.

  • The main backs up again within weeks of being cleared, even after jetting.
  • A sewage smell outdoors near the foundation or along the path of the line.
  • An unusually green stripe in the lawn, or a trench-shaped dip along the lateral route.
  • Several fixtures gurgle after heavy rain, a sign groundwater is getting in through a crack.
  • The cable comes back carrying roots, clay fragments or rust flakes.

Any of those makes a camera inspection of the lateral the right next step. Clearing the line again without looking only resets the clock.

What Fulton County Will and Will Not Do

The County's lateral flyer is short and specific. Public Works will help remove a blockage inside the right-of-way or easement only when three conditions are met. There has to be a serious threat to public health. A private plumbing professional hired by the owner has to be on site. And the owner has to sign a release holding the County harmless.

Repairs or new cleanouts inside the right-of-way or easement can be done by the County or a County-approved contractor, but the owner pays, and the County collects its fee before work begins. Outside the right-of-way, the County does not repair laterals under any circumstances.

In practice, your plumber handles the diagnosis, the private-side repair and the coordination with Fulton County when a defect sits under the street. If several homes on your street back up at once, that points to the main, and the North Fulton sewer after-hours line is the right call.

These rules apply the same way whether your water comes from the Roswell Water Utility or from Fulton County, because sewer service is Fulton County's across all of Roswell.

Septic Homes

Houses on Septic Near Roswell

Not every house near Roswell is on Fulton County sewer. The City of Milton notes that residents who pay no sewer bill are probably on septic, and parts of North Fulton with larger lots still are. A septic house can back up just like a sewer house, but the fix is different.

Between the house and the tank, the drain line can clog with roots, grease or a collapsed section, and that is plumbing work we handle. Inside the tank, a full or failing system needs a septic company to pump and inspect it. If the line clears and the backup returns within days, the tank or the drain field is the likely cause, and pumping comes before any more drain work.

Before You Approve Any Sewer Dig

A sound repair quote is built on evidence. Ask for these five things.

  • 1
    See the footage. You should be able to watch the defect on video, marked by its distance from the cleanout.
  • 2
    Get the depth and location. The locator reading shows how deep the pipe is and where it sits on your lot.
  • 3
    Know the pipe material. Clay, cast iron, Orangeburg or PVC changes the repair method and the fittings.
  • 4
    Ask about alternatives. If a dig is proposed, ask whether a point liner or full liner was considered, and why it was ruled out.
  • 5
    Make sure 811 is called. Georgia law requires buried utilities to be marked through Georgia 811 before any excavation.
