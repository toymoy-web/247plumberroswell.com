Drains and Sewer Lines

Everything from a slow bathroom sink to the lateral that runs under your front yard to the Fulton County main.

  • Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

    Kitchen, bath, laundry and main-line clogs cleared any hour, with a camera check when the same clog returns.

  • Hydro Jetting

    High-pressure cleaning for grease, scale and roots that keep coming back after a cable clears the middle.

  • Sewer Camera Inspection

    Recorded video of your drain or lateral, with defects located and marked from the surface.

  • Sewer Line Repair

    Spot repairs and point liners for cracked, separated or root-filled sections of the owner's lateral.

  • Trenchless Pipe Lining

    A cured-in-place liner that seals a round but damaged sewer pipe without trenching across the yard.

  • Sewer Line Replacement

    Full lateral replacement by open trench or pipe bursting when the old line is failing end to end.

Water Heaters

Tank, tankless and heat pump units, from a pilot that will not stay lit to a tank leaking across the garage floor.

  • Water Heater Repair

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements, thermostats and relief valves diagnosed and replaced when the tank itself is sound.

  • Replacing a Water Heater

    Right-sized replacement tanks with venting, expansion tank and permit questions answered before install day.

  • Tankless Units

    Descaling, error-code troubleshooting and repairs for on-demand units, plus conversions from tank to tankless.

Leaks and Water Lines

Hidden leaks, failing supply pipe and the buried line between the meter and your foundation.

  • Leak Detection

    Meter tests, acoustic listening and thermal imaging to pinpoint owner-side leaks before any wall is opened.

  • Leaks Under a Slab

    Finding and fixing supply leaks under a concrete slab by spot repair or rerouting the line overhead.

  • Burst Pipe Repair

    Emergency shutoff help and permanent repairs for split supply lines and failed fittings, day or night.

  • Whole-Home Repiping

    Replacing polybutylene and corroded galvanized supply systems with PEX or copper on a planned schedule.

  • Buried Water Service Lines

    Repairs to the buried service line you own, from the meter box to where the pipe enters the house.

  • Crawl Space Plumbing & Leak Repair

    Leaks, sagging drains and damp insulation under raised-floor homes, a common setup on Roswell's sloped lots.

How to Choose the Right Page

If water or sewage is somewhere it should not be right now, skip the reading and call. For anything else, start with the symptom. A slow drain belongs under drains, a spinning water meter under leaks, lukewarm showers under water heaters.

A few services overlap on purpose. Leak detection finds the problem, then slab leak repair, water line repair or repiping fixes it. Sewer camera inspection decides between sewer repair, lining and replacement. The pages link to each other where that hand-off happens.

What to do before you call

  • If water is spreading, close the main shutoff where the line enters the house.
  • If you smell gas, leave first and call 911 or Atlanta Gas Light from outside.
  • If sewage is coming up a drain, stop using water anywhere in the house.
